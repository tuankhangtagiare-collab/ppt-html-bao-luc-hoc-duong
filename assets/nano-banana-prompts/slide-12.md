## 🎨 Master Prompt for Nano Banana

```text
A panoramic, emotionally inspiring 3D cinematic scene of a modern, sunlit school campus at sunrise. A diverse group of teenage students, teachers, and school counselors are walking forward together across a wide, sun-drenched green school plaza with genuine smiles, supportive gestures, and shared conversations. An expansive glass school building rises into the clear dawn sky, reflecting golden sunlight. A giant, subtle translucent dome of gentle warm light encompasses the entire school community, symbolizing peace, safety, mutual respect, and unity.

- Composition: Epic wide panorama, low angle looking up slightly, characters walking forward towards the camera with warmth and optimism.
- Characters: 6-8 stylized 3D students and educators, diverse personalities, natural friendly expressions, modern uniforms with casual individuality.
- Environment: State-of-the-art sustainable school campus, lush green trees, sparkling fountains, clear architectural glass facades.
- Lighting: Glorious golden hour sunrise, warm sunburst flare at top-left, gentle rim light illuminating hair and shoulders, uplifting morning mist.
- Color Palette: Rich emerald greens (#10b981), warm golden honey (#f59e0b), sky cyan, crisp white, deep navy grounded shadows.
- Camera: Cinematic 28mm wide lens, shallow depth creating focus on the forward-looking group, radiant golden bokeh.
- Style: Pixar/Disney hyper-detailed animated feature still, architectural illustration, emotionally resonant educational finale.
- Aspect Ratio: 16:9 landscape.
- Negative Instructions: text, typography, darkness, gloomy shadows, sad faces, hostility, oversaturated neon, distorted hands.
```
